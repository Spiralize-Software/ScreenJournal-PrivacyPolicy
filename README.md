# Spiralize Software Privacy Policy
[Read here]()

## Summary
- Your screen activity, sleep estimates, and journal notes stay on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, and we never receive this data.
- The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device identifiers and usage information to serve and measure ads.
- Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play or the App Store. - Premium removes ads, and the ad service is not started at all.
You can delete your history at any time in the app, and uninstalling removes everything the app stored.
