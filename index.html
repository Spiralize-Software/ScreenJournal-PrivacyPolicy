PRIVACY POLICY

Effective October 23, 2026 · Developer: Spiralize Software

In short
  • Your screen activity, sleep estimates, and journal notes stay on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, and we never receive this data.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device identifiers and usage information to serve and measure ads.
  • Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play or the App Store. Premium removes ads, and the ad service is not started at all.
  • You can delete your history at any time in the app, and uninstalling removes everything the app stored.

1. Who we are

Screen Journal (“the app”) is developed by Spiralize Software (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions can be sent to spiralize.software@proton.me.

2. Information that stays on your device

To show your screen habits, the app records the following and stores it only in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • Screen events: the times your screen turns on, turns off, and is unlocked.
  • Derived insights: daily screen time, wake-up counts, and estimated sleep periods calculated from those events.
  • Journal entries: the tags and notes you choose to add to a day.
  • Preferences: theme, color palette, daily goal, and sleep settings.

This information is not uploaded, shared, or sold, and we cannot see it. The app does not read the content of your screen, your messages, your contacts, or which other apps you use.

If you use Copy daily summary or Copy screen events, the app copies that data to your device’s clipboard. Where it goes from there is up to you.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version of the app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device’s advertising ID (Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed and tapped, and diagnostic information

Google uses this information to deliver, measure, and improve ads, to prevent fraud, and, if you allow it, to personalize ads. Google’s handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • Consent: if you are in a region that requires it (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or certain US states), the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings under Privacy → Ads. On iOS you can limit tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • No ads: with Premium, the ad SDK is not started and no ad data is collected by the app.

4. Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your payment details. The store gives the app a confirmation of your purchase, and the app remembers on your device that Premium is unlocked so that it keeps working offline.

6. How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • Screen activity is kept on your device for 30 days on the free plan and up to 365 days with Premium. Older records are deleted automatically.
  • You can delete all history and journal entries at any time in Settings → Data & demo → Explore with demo data → Clear all history.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data it stored on your device.
  • Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google’s policies. You can manage it through your Google account and the advertising ID controls above.

7. Security

Your journal data is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. Communication between the ad and purchase services and their servers is encrypted in transit.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you have concerns, contact us at spiralize.software@proton.me.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because the app’s own data never leaves your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the app as described above. For advertising data, use the ad choices in section 3 or contact Google. For anything else, contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects or how it is used, we will update this page and its effective date, and describe significant changes in the app’s release notes.
